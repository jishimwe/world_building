matter form is orthogonal to the arc. it affects activation cost and cycle time at any scale but does not determine what scale is reachable. a personal kit can run on solid-phase substrate slowly; matter form refinement makes it faster and more efficient, not possible in the first place. matter form develops in the refinery, not in the apparatus. it is not marked on cells because it applies uniformly as a multiplier across all of them.
